About Me

I am Sunesh Praveen Raja Sundarasami, an M.Sc. student in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) in Sankt Augustin, Germany.

Since April 2026 I have been a working student at the August-Wilhelm Scheer Institute (AWSi), where I work with vision-language models, knowledge graphs and time-series models on industrial and energy problems. Before that I was a student research intern at Fraunhofer IVI, where I developed VoxelFix for correcting 3D semantic maps of aerial scenes.

Before my master's I spent four years in industry, as a data scientist at ADF Data Science in Chennai and as a senior analyst at Kantar in Bangalore.
